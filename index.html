    When you get a notification

    The check runs on weekdays around 4:20 PM ET, after the close. You are notified when the verdict changes, when a close crosses your level or support, or when the close lands within 2% of either line. On quiet days nothing is sent.

    A bullish confirmation needs all three: a close above your level, heavy volume, and a close above the 50-day average. A bearish one is the mirror image below support.

    A checklist for your own review, not advice to buy or sell. Prices come from a market-data service and can lag.
